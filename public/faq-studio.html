How It Works

Quest publication, participation, moderation, and payout terms

1. General Provisions

Questera is designed for publishing and completing quests. Before any payout, the result is verified by GenLayer validators, and rewards are paid out from escrow. By using the platform, you agree to these rules.

2. Participant Requirements

Participants must complete tasks exactly according to the quest description and submit truthful information. Spam, artificial boosting, using bots to submit solutions, and any attempts to manipulate verification outcomes through unfair methods are prohibited.

3. Quest Publication

The quest creator must clearly describe what the quest is about, provide prompt for AI, set up how long submissions are accepted, and the reward amount deposited into escrow. Quests with misleading wording may be removed by the administration.

During publication, the server deploys the escrow contract on Base Sepolia, and sets up the quest logic on GenLayer. After that, the escrow contract should be funded and then the quest becomes available for participation.

4. Completion Verification

A consensus verdict from GenLayer validators checks whether the submitted answers meets the quest requirements. If the result does not comply, the submission may be rejected with a stated reason.

5. Payouts and Liability

Payout is made after completion is confirmed (the verified result is accepted). The platform may restrict access for users who violate the rules, attempt to manipulate verification, or are found to be involved in fraudulent activity.

The reward is held in escrow from the moment the quest is published and is released only after successful completion.

6. How to Publish a Quest

1) Connect your wallet and make sure the required network is available for deployment. 2) Open the quest creation page. 3) Fill in: title, description/requirements, prompt, reward pool, end date, and upload an image. 4) Click “Create quest”. The server prepares the data, uploads the cover image, and initiates contract deployment.

7. How to Participate in Quests

1) Connect your wallet. 2) Open the quest page and carefully read the description and the end date. 3) Prepare your answer/solution and submit it through the participation form before the deadline. 4) Wait for the verification status. If the solution is accepted, you become eligible for a payout from escrow.

Submit original and well-justified solutions. Do not use bots, and do not try to replace or re-submit results in an attempt to bypass acceptance criteria.

8. Moderation and Safety

The administration may remove or hide quests that violate the rules or contain inaccurate/misleading information. We also fight spam and attempts to manipulate verification outcomes.

If you believe your submission was rejected incorrectly, review the wording of the requirements and your answer format. For correct acceptance, the provided criteria must be clear and unambiguous.
