How It Works

Participation, Moderation, and Payout Terms

1. General Provisions

The Questera platform is intended for publishing and completing quests, with result verification and subsequent reward allocation. By using the service, the user accepts these rules.

2. Participant Requirements

Participants must provide accurate information and complete tasks in accordance with their descriptions. Spam activity, artificial boosting, use of bots, and any methods of manipulating results are prohibited.

3. Quest Publication

The quest creator must clearly describe the completion requirements, acceptance criteria, deadlines, and reward amount. Quests with misleading terms may be removed by the administration.

4. Completion Verification

A consensus of GenLayer AI validators checks whether the submitted result meets the task requirements. In case of non-compliance, the submission may be rejected with a stated reason.

5. Payouts and Liability

Payment is made after completion is confirmed. The platform reserves the right to restrict access for users who violate the rules.
